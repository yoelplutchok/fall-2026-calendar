    The six things that can hurt you most

    1. PD and OMM each carry the same absence ladder, and both come off the final grade. One unexcused lab absence −5 points, two −10 points, three means “U” in PD and course failure in OMM. In both, forgetting to sign in counts as an unexcused absence; in OMM, so does wrong attire, leaving early, or arriving more than 5 minutes late.
    2. Histology: 75% of the grade sits on three exams with no dates published.
    3. PME video assignment: 10 points off per day late, and the syllabus says “No exceptions” on the Oct 26 deadline.
    4. Attendance and participation points are 5–15% in every course. Showing up is worth more than most exams’ margin.
    5. OMM grade complaints die after 3 business days. Anatomy attendance challenges die after 1 week. Everything else gets 3 weeks.
    6. Remediating 11 or more credits in one year means academic dismissal. Anatomy alone is 6.5 credits; Anatomy plus any one of Biochemistry or Physiology is 11.5.
