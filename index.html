    The five things that can hurt you most

    1. PD: three unexcused skills-lab absences = automatic “U” for the course. One costs 5 points off your final grade, two costs 10. Forgetting to sign in counts as an absence.
    2. Histology: 75% of the grade sits on three exams with no dates published.
    3. PME video assignment: 10 points off per day late, and the syllabus says “No exceptions” on the Oct 26 deadline.
    4. Attendance points are 5–15% in six courses. Showing up is worth more than most exams’ margin.
    5. OMM grade complaints die after 3 business days. Everything else gets 3 weeks.
