How to use this guide

Part one runs through all nineteen weeks. Each week lists the sessions, what to watch before each one, anything due, the topics being taught, and the resources to work in priority order. Part two does the same for each of the eight exams, gathered in one place.

  • Numbered lists are ordered by priority. Item 1 is what to do first if time is short.
  • Start every module from the faculty's own topic and disease lists. They are two or three pages long, written as testable objectives, and the disease lists are the option pools for the diagnosis questions.
  • Study time should follow exam points. Foundations 2 is 44 points and GI is 8, so the two are not worth equal hours even though far more practice material exists for GI.
  • Every lecture number in the Topics panel is a link to that lecture's PowerPoint on Canvas, and so is every primer. The primer numbers on the Canvas filenames belong to an older sequence and do not match the syllabus — syllabus Primer 9 Acid-Base is the file called Primer 01 Acids and Bases, and syllabus Primer 1 Thermodynamics is Primer 04_Kamath_Thermodynamics. The links below use the syllabus numbering and point at the right file.
  • Each week closes with the rest of the archive for that week's material. Those lists are complete: every catalogued document whose questions route to those lectures is in them, from 2018 to 2026 and from all three campuses, ranked by measured question yield. Nothing relevant has been left out, and what is not worth opening is named rather than hidden.
  • Links. Canvas links open a file download and need a Canvas login. Drive links point to the SGA upperclassmen archive and need the @student.touro.edu Google account — if a link is refused, switch accounts or insert /u/1 after the domain.
