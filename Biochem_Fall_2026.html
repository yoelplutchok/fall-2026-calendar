Weekly guide Course information

How to use this guide

Part one runs through all nineteen weeks. Each week lists the sessions, what to watch before each one, anything due, and the resources to work in priority order. Part two does the same for each of the eight exams, gathered in one place.

  • Numbered lists are ordered by priority. Item 1 is what to do first if time is short.
  • Start every module from the faculty's own topic and disease lists. They are two or three pages long, written as testable objectives, and the disease lists are the option pools for the diagnosis questions.
  • Study time should follow exam points. Foundations 2 is 44 points and GI is 8, so the two are not worth equal hours even though far more practice material exists for GI.
  • Links. Canvas links open a file download and need a Canvas login. Drive links point to the SGA upperclassmen archive and need the @student.touro.edu Google account — if a link is refused, switch accounts or insert /u/1 after the domain.
