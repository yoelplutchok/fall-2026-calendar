Biochemistry and medical genetics are taught as one course. Lectures 1–12 and 22–42 are biochemistry, taught against Lippincott; lectures 13–21 are medical genetics, taught against Thompson & Thompson and assessed through patient cases. Both halves are examined inside the eight shared module exams.

The course holds 42 lecture videos, 14 short primers, 13 clinical vignettes and one team-based learning session. Videos are in the Canvas Media Gallery and slides are in the weekly modules.

An exam point is worth roughly forty quiz points, and a single interactive-session check-in is worth about as much as a whole quiz. This is why the study guide allocates time by exam point value rather than by how much practice material exists for a module.

  • The 8% is attendance in practice. Answering a polling question incorrectly costs nothing; not being in the room costs the point. Roughly 21 sessions carry one or two points each.
  • Quiz deadlines are set per quiz on Canvas, not on a weekly cycle. Each quiz unlocks about two weeks ahead and closes at 11:59 PM on the day of the exam it belongs to. Attempt limits vary from three to unlimited. Read both on the individual quiz.
  • The running total shown in Canvas is computed on raw points and will not match the 90/8/2 split. The percentages above are what the final grade is calculated from. Final grades are posted to TouroOne; the grading scale is in the Student Handbook.
  • Around thirty ungraded practice quizzes are also available, with unlimited attempts.

How the week runs

  • Interactive sessions assume the week's videos have been watched. Ten to fifteen minutes of questions and answers, then thirty-five to forty minutes of polling questions. Attendance counts.
  • Supplemental sessions review the week and take student questions. Optional and ungraded, but in the week before each exam they become exam reviews.
  • Clinical vignettes present a patient with history, physical, differential and labs. The task is to identify the biochemical or genetic basis of the disorder and propose management.
  • All sessions are in person, with no remote option. Attendance is required by college policy, and an excused absence needs a request form filed with the Office of Student Affairs.
The syllabus names Poll Everywhere as the in-class polling tool and the orientation deck names iClicker. Install and log in to both before the first session on Aug 4, and confirm which one the course uses in the room.
