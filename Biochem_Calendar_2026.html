  • Four further quizzes in the gradebook carry 2025 dates and are not on this year's schedule — a Week 2 Pedigree Inheritance quiz, a Week 2 quiz marked F25, a Week 7 Regulation of Gene Expression quiz, and a duplicate Week 15 Vitamins and Iron quiz.
